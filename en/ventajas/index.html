Advantages

DESARROLLO PERSONAL:
IAESTE represents an excellent opportunity to prepare for your professional future. Not only will you learn how to work nationally, but you will also develop your presentation, event management, human resources, information technology and public relations skills. Take advantage of the opportunity to develop in what you are passionate about!

INTERNATIONALIZATION:
With IAESTE, the limits go far beyond. Access an international network of students, paid internships, amazing experiences and events. IAESTE is an exchange program in a globalized environment where students from around the globe share their interests and collaborate on projects to work towards common goals.

CUMPLE TUS METAS:
IAESTE gives you the opportunity not only to fulfill your dreams abroad, but also to contribute to helping other students complete their professional development. Be part of a team dedicated to helping others.

EXPAND YOUR NETWORK:
At IAESTE, you can expand your connections and broaden your professional network. In addition, you will connect with people from all continents, experiencing the cultural differences that enrich exchange and coexistence. Through our joint work and our mission, our volunteers establish lasting friendships around the world
