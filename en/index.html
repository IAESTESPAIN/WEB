What is IAESTE?

IAESTE (International Association for the Exchange of Students for Technical Experience ) is an international non-profit association present in over 80 countries. It represents the interests of students, companies and universities through paid internships abroad. Each year, hundreds of students have the opportunity to expand their professional and personal knowledge by working in companies around the world for periods ranging from one month to one year.
